A commonplace book for everything you read

Share an article, a post, a few screenshots or a PDF to Sangrah, and it keeps the clean text in a library you can search, read, listen to and quote from. संग्रह means a collection. With Sangrah Pro, each save also gets a Key Idea and summary cards, so you remember what it said.

What it does

  • Save from any app

    Tap Share and choose Sangrah: links from Safari, X, Instagram, LinkedIn, Substack, Medium, YouTube and most websites; up to 10 screenshots, combined into one save; or a PDF, read on your iPhone.

  • Articles you pay for, as you see them

    Share a story from Safari while you’re signed in, and Sangrah saves the article text on your screen, so you keep the full piece.

  • Key Idea and summary cards

    With Sangrah Pro, each save gets its Key Idea, then 5 to 7 summary cards in Claude’s words that cover the piece in order, with a summary, key points and tags.

  • Quote cards to share

    Select a passage and choose Save as Quote. Share any quote or card as an image, with a caption carrying the title, author and link. Every bookmark is in the Quotes tab.

  • A clean PDF of every save

    Open a tidy PDF of any save, then share it or save it to Files.

  • Listen

    Sangrah reads your saves aloud with the voices on your iPhone, with a mini player and lock-screen controls.

  • Daily Quote widget

    A quote from your own saves on your Home Screen each day, with Shuffle for another.

  • Search that reads everything

    Library search matches titles, authors, the article text, your notes, tags, key points, summary cards and quotes.

  • Export your whole library

    Profile → Export Library makes one zip of every save, as text files you can open anywhere, to keep or send where you choose.

Free and Pro

  • Free

    Up to 5 saves a day, with the clean text, the PDF, Listen, search, your own categories, notes, quotes, the widget and Export Library. No AI: on the Free plan, nothing is sent to Claude.

  • Sangrah Pro

    Smart summaries on your saves: the Key Idea, summary cards, key points, tags and automatic categories, written by Claude once you allow it. Also unlimited saves, screenshots read by Claude, and Compose, which brings several saves together.

  • Price

    In India, Sangrah Pro is ₹299 a month or ₹2,499 a year, and the first month is free. It’s billed by the App Store, and you can cancel any time. Smart summaries have a fair-use limit of 300 a month.

Private by design

A plain summary. The privacy policy is the full account.

Your library is yours
Your saves, notes, quotes, screenshots and PDFs are locked to your account, stored with Supabase in its Mumbai region in India. A PDF opens only for you.
Claude only when you allow it
Smart summaries are part of Sangrah Pro, and Sangrah asks before any of your content goes to Anthropic’s Claude API. On the Free plan, nothing is sent to Claude, and under Anthropic’s commercial terms the data isn’t used to train its models.
Read on your iPhone
PDFs you share, and screenshots on the free plan, are read on your iPhone, and only their text is sent to Sangrah.
Listen stays on your iPhone
Saves are read aloud by the voices on your iPhone, so nothing is sent anywhere to do it.
Payments through Apple
Sangrah Pro is bought and billed through the App Store. Sangrah never sees your payment details.
No ads, analytics or tracking
Nothing tracks you across other apps or websites, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
