Questions, problems and privacy requests.

Contact

Email Ateeshay.jain@gmail.com. In the app, Profile → Contact Support opens an email with your app version filled in, which helps us find the problem faster. If the problem is with your account or plan, also paste your account ID: in Profile, tap Account ID to copy it.

Common questions

How do I save something?

In any app, tap Share and choose Sangrah. If Sangrah isn’t in the row of apps, scroll to the end, tap More and add it. You can also tap + in the Library and paste a link.

Can I save screenshots?

Yes. Select up to 10 screenshots in Photos, tap Share and choose Sangrah. They’re combined into one save. On the free plan, your iPhone reads them. With Pro, once you’ve allowed it, Claude reads them and writes a smart summary.

Can I save a PDF?

Yes. Share one PDF to Sangrah. Your iPhone reads its text, and only the text is sent to Sangrah. PDFs that are locked with a password, or scans with no readable text, can’t be saved.

Can I save an article I pay for?

Yes. Open it in Safari while you’re signed in, tap Share and choose Sangrah. Sangrah saves the article text on your screen, so you get the full story as you see it.

What is Ask?

Ask is a chat about what you’ve saved. Tap Ask at the top of the Library, or Engage with this article on a save to talk about that one, and type a question, like “What have I saved about building habits?” Then follow up, like “What does the second one say?” Claude answers from your saves first and may search the web when they don’t cover it, with links to the saves and pages it used. Only your question and your earlier questions go to the web search, never your saves. Your chats are kept with your account under Chats, where you can reopen them or swipe them away. A chat about one save is deleted with that save, and all chats with your account. Ask is part of Sangrah Pro, with unlimited everyday chat within fair use and 60 deep answers a month (critique, debate and the other deep moves), and Sangrah asks before anything is sent to Claude.

Is Sangrah free?

Yes. You can save up to 5 articles a day and keep the clean text, the PDF, Listen, search, your own categories, notes, the widget and Export Library, all free. On the Free plan, nothing is sent to Claude. Sangrah Pro adds smart summaries with a Key Idea, summary cards, tags and automatic categories, unlimited saves, screenshots read by Claude, Compose and Ask. In India it’s ₹499 a month or ₹4,999 a year, and the first month is free. Pro has monthly limits: 60 smart summaries, 60 deep answers in Ask and 5 Compose runs (15, 15 and 1 during the free month). After the smart summary limit, saves are kept and get their summary the next month. Pro is the only purchase; there are no add-ons.

Why is a save incomplete?

Some sites block readers or only show the full text after you sign in. If you can read it in Safari, share it from there. Screenshots of the page are a good fallback.

How do I get a copy of my library?

In Profile, tap Export Library. Sangrah makes a zip of every save, as text files you can open anywhere, and opens the share sheet so you can save it to Files or send it. The zip is deleted from your iPhone when you close the share sheet.

How do I delete a save?

Open it, tap ⋯ and choose Delete. Its PDF and screenshots are deleted with it.

Your plan and your account

Restore Purchases

If you bought Sangrah Pro and the app shows the free plan, open Profile and tap Restore Purchases. It’s also at the bottom of the Upgrade to Pro screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Cancel Sangrah Pro

In Profile, tap Manage Subscription, or go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Delete your account

In Profile, tap Delete Account. This removes your account and everything in it: saves, quotes, notes, categories, PDFs and screenshots. It can’t be undone, so use Export Library first if you want a copy. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel Sangrah Pro, so cancel it first.
