2. What Sangrah does

Sangrah saves what you read, from links, screenshots and PDFs, into a clean, searchable library on your account. You can read a save as clean text or a PDF, listen to it, keep quotes, and, on Sangrah Pro, get smart summaries written by AI.

4. Free and Pro

Free. Up to 5 saves a day. The day resets at midnight UTC (5:30 am in India). Free includes the clean text, the PDF, Listen, search, your own categories, notes, the widget, the Weekly Summary and Export Library. Screenshots and PDFs are read on your iPhone. On the Free plan, nothing is sent to Claude. After 5 saves in a day, Sangrah takes no more saves until the next day. Your existing saves aren’t affected.

Sangrah Pro. Everything in Free, plus:

  • unlimited saves;
  • smart summaries on your saves: a Key Idea, a short and a long summary, key points, summary cards, tags and automatic categories;
  • screenshots read by Claude;
  • Compose, which writes something new from several of your saves;
  • Ask, a chat about your saves. Claude answers from your saves first and may search the web when a question needs it.

Smart summaries, screenshots read by Claude, Compose and Ask only run after you allow Sangrah to send your content to Anthropic’s Claude. Until you allow it, your saves are kept without a summary.

Fair-use limits on Pro. Each Pro feature that uses Claude costs us money every time it runs, so Pro has monthly limits. Months are calendar months in UTC.

  • Smart summaries: up to 60 a month. After that, your saves are still kept, with their text, and get their summary the next month. Nothing is lost.
  • Compose: up to 5 runs a month. After that, Compose is available again the next month.
  • Ask: up to 60 messages a month. Every message you send counts as one, follow-up questions included. After that, Ask is available again the next month.
  • During the free first month, the limits are lower: 15 smart summaries, 15 Ask messages and 1 Compose run.

Pro is the only purchase. There are no add-ons or extra packs.

We may change these limits. If we lower a limit, we will say so in the app before the change applies.
