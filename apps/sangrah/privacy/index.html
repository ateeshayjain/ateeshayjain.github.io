Sangrah

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026.

What Sangrah stores

Your account: your email address and your account ID, which Profile shows so you can copy it, for example when you contact us. With Sign in with Apple, your email can be a private address from Apple, and Sangrah can keep a token from Apple, used only to end Sangrah’s access when you delete your account. What you save: links, the text Sangrah reads from them, your notes, categories, tags and quotes, and, on Sangrah Pro, the smart summaries and summary cards written for your saves. Sangrah also stores the PDFs it makes from your saves, which plan you’re on, and how many saves, summaries, Compose runs and Ask messages you’ve used. Your Ask chats are stored with your account: your questions, the answers, and the saves and web pages each answer used.

How your saves are read

To read a link, Sangrah fetches the page directly or through a reader service (Jina Reader for articles and Instagram posts, FxTwitter and X’s embed service for posts on X, YouTube for a video’s title). When you share an article from Safari, Sangrah also sends the text on your screen, so a story you’re signed in to is saved as you see it. PDFs you share are read on your iPhone, and only their text is sent to Sangrah. Screenshots are read on your iPhone in the same way, unless you’re on Sangrah Pro and have allowed Claude to read them.

Sangrah Pro and Claude

Smart summaries are part of Sangrah Pro, and Sangrah asks before any of your content goes to Anthropic’s Claude API: when your plan first turns Pro, and again before Summarise Now, Compose or Ask if you haven’t allowed it. Until you tap Allow, nothing is sent to Claude, and your saves are kept without a summary. Once you allow it, the title and text of each save (up to its first 40,000 characters), or its screenshots, and the names of your tags are sent to Claude to write the smart summary, Key Idea, key points, summary cards and tags. To withdraw it, turn off Smart summaries with Claude in Profile: from then on nothing more is sent to Claude, and everything you’ve already saved, summaries included, is kept. Screenshots are deleted from Sangrah once Claude has read them. Compose sends the text of the saves you pick. Ask is a chat. Each message sends your question, your earlier messages in that chat, and excerpts from the saves that match it (in a chat about one save, that save’s text) to Claude, only on Sangrah Pro and only once you allow it. Ask may also search the web when a question needs it, through Anthropic’s web search. Only your question and your earlier questions go to the search, and in a chat about one save, its title, author and site. Your saves are never sent to the search. Answers show which saves and web pages they used. Your email, account ID and notes aren’t sent. On the Free plan, nothing is sent to Claude. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, this data isn’t used to train its models.

Where your data is processed

Your account and saves are stored with Supabase, in its Mumbai region in India. Supabase also handles sign-in. Some processing happens outside India: Claude runs in the United States, and the reader services run on their own servers, which may be in other countries.

What stays on your iPhone

Listen uses the voices on your iPhone, so nothing is sent anywhere to read a save aloud. The quotes in the widget, your Listen settings and your answer about Claude are kept on your iPhone. A link you share while offline waits on your iPhone until Sangrah can send it. Export Library makes a zip of your whole library on your iPhone for you to save or send where you choose; Sangrah deletes it from your iPhone when you close the share sheet, and when you sign out.

Who can see it

Only you. Your saves, notes, quotes, screenshots and PDFs are locked to your account. A PDF opens only for you, through a link that stops working after a few minutes. When you share a PDF, a quote card or your exported library, you send a copy, and whoever gets it can keep it.

Subscriptions

Sangrah Pro is bought and billed through the App Store. Sangrah never sees your payment details. Each purchase carries your account ID, so Apple’s record of it names your account, and each one is confirmed with Apple. Sangrah stores which plan you’re on, when it renews or ends, and the App Store’s ID for your purchase, and checks your subscription’s status with Apple.

What Sangrah doesn’t do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking across other apps or websites, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Deleting your data

To delete a save, open it, tap ⋯ and choose Delete. Its PDF, screenshots and any Ask chat about it are deleted with it. To delete a chat yourself, open Chats in Ask and swipe it away. To delete your account and everything in it, including your saves, quotes, notes, categories, PDFs, screenshots and Ask chats, go to Profile and tap Delete Account. Deleting can’t be undone, so to keep a copy, use Profile → Export Library first. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel Sangrah Pro, so cancel it first in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
